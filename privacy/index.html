NEO Poster – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

NEO Poster is a desktop application made and used only by the NEO team to schedule and publish NEO's own content (videos and images about the Roblox experience NEO) to NEO's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Pinterest accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it is not used to access anyone else's accounts.

1. WHAT NEO POSTER ACCESSES

YouTube: permission to upload videos to NEO's own channel and to read the channel's name.

TikTok: permission to read the linked account's basic profile (display name and avatar) and to send videos to that account's drafts inbox.

Instagram: permission to read the linked professional account's basic profile and to publish Reels and pictures to it.

Pinterest: permission to read NEO's own boards and to create Pins on them.

NEO Poster does not access, collect or store any information about other users of these platforms, or about Roblox players.

2. WHAT NEO POSTER STORES, AND WHERE

The sign-in tokens each platform provides, so scheduled posts can be published.

The schedule, captions, titles and hashtags the NEO team writes for its own posts.

All of this is stored only on the NEO team's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server run by NEO, and there is no NEO Poster database or cloud service.

3. HOW THE INFORMATION IS USED

Only to publish NEO's own content to NEO's own accounts at the times the NEO team chooses.

It is never sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to build profiles of anyone.

4. DATA SENT TO PLATFORMS

Content is sent directly to Google (YouTube), TikTok, Meta (Instagram) and Pinterest through their official APIs when it is published. Their own privacy policies apply to content once it is on their platforms.

5. REMOVING ACCESS AND DELETING DATA

YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions.

Instagram: Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites.

Pinterest: Settings > Security and logins (or Apps).

Deleting the NEO Poster folder from the computer deletes everything NEO Poster has stored. Deletion requests can also be sent to the contact below.

6. CHILDREN

NEO Poster is not used by, directed at, or collecting information from children or any other users. It is an internal tool for the NEO team only.

7. CHANGES

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same link with a new effective date.
