NEO Poster – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

NEO Poster is a desktop application made and used only by the NEO team to schedule and publish NEO's own content (videos and images about the Roblox experience NEO) to NEO's own Pinterest and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it is not used to access anyone else's accounts.

1. What NEO Poster accesses

Pinterest: when the NEO account owner signs in, NEO Poster receives permission to read that account's own boards and to create Pins on that account's boards.

YouTube: when the NEO account owner signs in, NEO Poster receives permission to upload videos to that account's own channel and to read the channel's name.

NEO Poster does not access, collect, or store any information about other Pinterest users, YouTube users, or Roblox players.

2. What NEO Poster stores, and where

The sign-in tokens Pinterest and Google provide, so scheduled posts can be published.

The schedule, captions, titles, and hashtags the NEO team writes for its own posts.

All of this is stored only on the NEO team's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server run by NEO, and there is no NEO Poster database or cloud service.

3. How the information is used

Only to publish NEO's own content to NEO's own Pinterest and YouTube accounts at the times the NEO team chooses.

It is never sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to build profiles of anyone.

4. Data sent to Pinterest and Google

Content is sent directly to Pinterest and Google (YouTube) through their official APIs when it is published. Their own privacy policies apply to content once it is on their platforms.

5. Removing access and deleting data

Pinterest access can be removed at any time in Pinterest Settings > Security and logins (or Apps).

YouTube access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting the NEO Poster folder from the computer deletes everything NEO Poster has stored.

6. Children

NEO Poster is not used by, directed at, or collecting information from children or any other users. It is an internal tool for the NEO team only.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same link with a new effective date.
